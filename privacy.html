Hermes Backup - Privacy Policy

Hermes Backup is a personal backup tool. It uses Google Drive only to store and restore backups of its owner's own server in his own Google account.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data is kept only in the user's own Google Drive and on the owner's server.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: through the owner's GitHub profile.
